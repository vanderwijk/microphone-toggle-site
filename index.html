Why it exists

Close the MacBook.
Keep the conversation open.

A closed MacBook can leave its built-in microphones hidden behind your display or far from your voice. Your iPhone is often right beside you—and its microphone is excellent. Microphone Toggle makes that setup practical with one menu bar click.

Support

Simple by design.

Connect your iPhone through Continuity Camera, then click the menu bar icon to switch. Right-click for device selection, launch at login and app information.

My iPhone does not appear

Check that Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are enabled, both devices use the same Apple Account, and Continuity Camera is enabled on your iPhone.

Does the app record audio?

No. It only asks macOS to change the default input device. It never receives or stores audio.

How do I restore it after reinstalling my Mac?

Download it again from your Mac App Store purchase history. Because it is free and has no account, there is nothing else to restore.

Privacy policy · 14 August 2026

Zero data collected.

Microphone Toggle does not collect, store, process or share personal data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud services or third-party SDKs. It does not record or access microphone audio. Device preferences are stored only on your Mac using Apple's standard local preferences system.
